Extraction

AI image-extraction platform — scoped, shipped, and still operated for Swift ProSys.

Sole engineer and client contact, from the first scoping call to production support. The platform replaced manual document and image data entry for language-specific teams — a cross-platform desktop app on a self-hosted Supabase/Postgres backend, with encrypted key vaulting, role-based access, and over-the-air updates.

Work routes across five model providers — OpenAI, Google Gemini, Qwen, Vector Engine and local Ollama — behind one interface, so workloads move on price or capability. Template-driven extraction lets non-technical operators add new document types without engineering involvement, and per-project cost analytics make spend predictable before a run.

Outcome

~200 people use it daily · 6,000,000,000 tokens in five months · 5 LLM providers behind one interface
