A bit about me.

Data Engineer at Statista in Hamburg. I started there as a working student in 2022, moved through analyst roles, and now work on internal data tools and the pipelines behind them. Most recently I built our news intelligence stack — ingestion, deduplication, embeddings, and vector search over large indexes.

On the other side, I'm an AI Consultant and Engineer at Swift ProSys, where I built their image extraction platform end to end — a cross-platform desktop app on a self-hosted Supabase/Postgres backend, with multi-provider LLM routing, template-driven extraction, encrypted key vaulting, role-based access, per-project cost tracking, and over-the-air updates.

Right now most of my curiosity goes to agentic coding and workflows — building retrieval agents, and figuring out how to spec, review, and evaluate what agents produce so it's actually safe to ship.
